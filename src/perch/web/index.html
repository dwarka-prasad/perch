Listening ports 
      

      

        
      

      
PortBind address
      ExposureProcessPID
      Stop the process

      

      
Stop asks the
      listener to shut down (SIGTERM); Force kills it outright (SIGKILL) and
      loses any unsaved state. Both act on the exact PID shown, so a port that got
      re-bound in the meantime is never hit by mistake. Process names and the stop
      buttons show only for your own processes — Linux hides other users' processes
      without root, and for those Perch gives you the command to copy instead.
      0.0.0.0 / :: means the port accepts connections from the network, not just
      this machine.
